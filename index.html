Our Story

A culinary journey from the shores of the Aegean to the island of Bali

Where Turkey Meets Bali

Bodrum Corner was born from a passion for sharing the rich and vibrant flavors of Turkish cuisine with the world. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, bringing the warmth of Mediterranean hospitality to the tropical paradise of Bali.

Every dish we serve tells a story — from the sizzle of our charcoal-grilled kebabs to the delicate layers of our handmade baklava. We source the freshest local ingredients and blend them with imported Turkish spices to create an authentic dining experience you won't find anywhere else on the island.
